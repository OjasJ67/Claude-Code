Privacy

Short version: we collect as little as possible.

  • This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
  • When you tap Email Inquiry, what you typed in the form (your name, email and message) goes to Netlify, the company that hosts this site, which stores it and emails it to us. Text Inquiry and Call use your own phone. Either way, we only receive what you choose to send.
  • We use your details only to answer your inquiry and never sell them.
  • If you answer our survey, Google Forms stores your answers. If you give us your email there, we use it only to tell you when we launch, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
  • The chat assistant gives instant answers inside your browser and sends nothing. If we switch on AI answers, the questions you type in the chat are sent through our website to Anthropic, our AI provider, only to write a reply; we don't keep them. Please don't type personal details into the chat.
  • For privacy requests, email AgarAndOak@gmail.com.

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Nothing else is requested from other websites.
